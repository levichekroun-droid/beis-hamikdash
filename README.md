# Beis HaMikdash: The Avodah

A 3D browser game set in the Beis HaMikdash. You start as a Levi at the gates, become a Kohen, and rise to Kohen Gadol for the avodah of Yom Kippur.

Play it: https://levichekroun-droid.github.io/beis-hamikdash/

## Controls

- W A S D: walk
- Mouse: look around
- Shift: hurry
- E: do the avodah at the glowing marker
- A S D: pluck the kinor strings in the song
- V: switch between seeing your kohen and seeing through his eyes

The whole game is one file, `index.html`.
